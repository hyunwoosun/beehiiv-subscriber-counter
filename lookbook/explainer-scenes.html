Your own artwork, animated

Making one for a real video

Give Claude the script for the segment and say which scene type fits. It builds the scene to your timing, then renders it.

What to send

  • The lines you'll say over the scene, with rough timestamps
  • Your brand colors and fonts, if you have them
  • Any images or logos to include

What you get back

  • A live preview you can review and request changes on
  • A 1080×1920 MP4, rendered in Claude Code
  • A transparent or green-screen version, if it sits over your footage

Where it hits limits

  • No photoreal or AI-generated imagery
  • Characters stay simple, without lip-sync or expressive acting
  • 3D stays at the level of simple shapes, not detailed scenes
