Getting it into your edit

Everything above is code, so it renders frame-perfect at any resolution. These are the routes from a style you like to a clip on your timeline.

Pick a style, give Claude the content

"Use the word-pop captions for this script" or "make the flowchart for 가다 → 가요." Claude builds it at 1080×1920 in your fonts and colors.

Render to a video file

In Claude Code, Claude can capture the animation frame by frame with a headless browser and encode it with ffmpeg: .mp4 at 30 or 60 fps. In regular Claude chat you get the live preview, which you can screen-record.

Keep the background transparent

Overlays export as .mov ProRes 4444 or .webm with alpha for Premiere, Final Cut and DaVinci. If your editor can't read alpha video, render on solid green and key it out (CapCut's chroma key works for this).

Sync to your voice

Share word-level timestamps (an .srt from your editor's auto-captions, or a transcription tool) and captions, highlights and reveals can land on the exact word.

Turn a style into a template

Ask for a version where you only change the text, colors and timing per video, so each new reel takes minutes.

Mind the safe zones

Turn on "Safe zones" above. Reels, TikTok and Shorts cover roughly the bottom 18% with captions and the right edge with buttons, so key text belongs in the middle band.

Claude makes these well

  • Kinetic type, captions and labels
  • Charts, counters, diagrams and timelines
  • Icons, shapes, particles and simple 3D
  • UI mockups: chats, search bars, comment replies

Outside what Claude generates

  • Photoreal or AI-generated footage
  • Lip-synced or character animation with acting
  • Detailed 3D scenes and realistic lighting
  • Cutting your talking-head footage itself (your editor does that)
