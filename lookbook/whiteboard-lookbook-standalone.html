Whiteboard Sketch Lookbook

17 ways to explain a concept with hand-drawn visuals, all in one sketch style. Each card uses a different visual device: tables, Venn diagrams, decision trees, maps, comics, graphs and more. Switch the surface to see the same scenes on a chalkboard, a notebook, kraft paper or a lightboard. Tap a frame to pause it, or drag its scrubber.

Surface

Picking one for a video

Any of these devices can carry any topic. Tell Claude the point of the segment and which device fits, and it draws the scene to the timing of your voiceover.

Mix and match

  • Any device on any surface
  • Your own handwriting-style font, or a Korean hand font like Gaegu or Nanum Pen
  • Your brand colors as the marker colors

Timed to your voice

  • Each stroke and word can start on the exact word you say
  • Slower drawing for teaching, faster for hooks
  • The eraser wipe can be a transition between ideas

Export

  • 1080×1920 MP4, rendered frame by frame in Claude Code
  • Transparent version for a lightboard look over your footage
  • Still PNG of the finished board, for thumbnails or carousels
